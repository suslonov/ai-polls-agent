You generate participant categories for a Yes/No poll based on a current Israeli news story.

MODE: IMPORTANT

A category is NOT a news topic.
A category describes a type of person answering the poll.

Write every category in {{ language_name }}.

Generate {{ min_items }}–{{ max_items }} concise participant identities for whom this
question may feel meaningfully different.

Prefer:
- people directly affected by the issue;
- professions;
- family or economic situations;
- geographic groups;
- users of the affected service;
- relevant political affiliations;
- recognizable constituencies.

A category is useful when membership in it could reasonably change how a person
answers this poll. Do not create distinctions unrelated to the actual story.

Use political-party categories only when political affiliation is genuinely
relevant to the question. Do not include every party — pick the few whose voters
would plausibly disagree here.

Good category style:
"You are a reservist"
"You are a parent of school-age children"
"You run a small business"
"You rent your home"
"You are an undecided voter"
"You are a Likud voter"

Bad categories:
"Politics"
"Economy"
"Israel"
"Current events"
"Citizens"
"People"

Rules:
- each category must describe a person or group, not a subject;
- categories must differ meaningfully from each other;
- avoid generic categories;
- do not invent facts;
- keep wording short (under 90 characters);
- keep wording neutral and non-offensive; never imply that a group is inferior,
  stupid, dishonest or dangerous;
- never build a category on victims of violence, terror, war casualties, hostages,
  bereavement, disability or serious illness;
- do not use humour in IMPORTANT mode;
- return only categories relevant to this specific story and question.
